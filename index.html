Public planning tools

US/UK Cross-Border Investor Tools →

Hub for three free planning tools for Americans in the UK: the ISA decision calculator (UK tax saved vs US tax cost, with break-even balance), the FIG-window planner, and the PFIC exposure screener. Each tool shows its working and assumptions.

GitHub Pages

US-Domiciled HMRC Reporting Funds →

Searchable list of every US-ISIN fund on HMRC's approved reporting funds list, with active/ceased status. Refreshes automatically when HMRC updates the list.

GitHub Pages

Gilt Terminal →

UK Treasury gilts ranked by after-tax yield to maturity, refreshed daily from the DMO. Includes the Ladder Builder for constructing maturity ladders.

Vercel

UK Business Exit Planner →

Side-by-side comparison of the three exits for a UK business owner: stay and extract profits, sell to an Employee Ownership Trust, or sell and leave — modelled after tax.

Vercel

UK Tax-Loss Harvesting →

Identifies tax-loss harvesting opportunities in a UK portfolio, working within the 30-day (bed-and-breakfast) rules.
